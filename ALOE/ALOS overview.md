# ⭐ **Vision: A Complete Assisted Living Operating Environment (ALOE)**

PointClickCare (PCC) is the leading **clinical EMR** for skilled nursing and medically oriented workflows.
But **Assisted Living & Memory Care (AL/MC) are fundamentally operational businesses**, not clinical ones.

**The AL Operating Environment (ALOE)** fills the operational void that PCC does not cover:

* Daily task execution
* Family communication
* Incident workflows
* Compliance
* Engagement
* Staff allocation
* Facility operations
* Real-time oversight

This system becomes the **operational backbone** for AL/MC buildings — starting with one module and scaling to a full suite.

# **MODULE 1 — Care Task Automation & ADL Execution (MVP)**

### **Problem**

AL caregivers rely heavily on manual task sheets, whiteboards, memory, and inconsistent documentation.
This causes:

* Missed care
* No real-time oversight
* High turnover
* Poor survey readiness
* Zero consistency across buildings

**PCC Limitation:**
PCC’s ADL module is optimized for SNF clinical workflows. AL caregivers do not adopt it well.

### **Solution (ALOE)**

* Mobile-first, tap-to-complete tasks
* Real-time dashboards for supervisors
* Automatic assignment based on service plans
* Alerts for overdue tasks
* Easy onboarding for new staff

**Impact:**
Creates consistent, repeatable, measurable care delivery across an entire 300-building operation.
