# **Executive Summary: The Assisted Living Operating Environment (ALOE)**

### *A Strategic Expansion Platform That Complements PointClickCare and Scales Across 300 Facilities and Beyond*

---

## **The Vision**

Assisted Living communities run on hundreds of daily operational tasks—hydration, toileting, ADLs, safety checks, activities, family updates, incident follow-up, staffing coverage, and building operations.

**PointClickCare (PCC) is the clinical record.
What’s missing is the *Operational Record*.**

The **Assisted Living Operating Environment (ALOE)** fills this gap by standardizing how care is delivered, supervised, documented, and communicated across every shift and every building.

This is not an EMR competitor.
This is the **operational layer PCC does not provide**, designed specifically for AL/MC staffing models and workflows.
