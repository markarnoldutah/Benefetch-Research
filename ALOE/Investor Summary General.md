# **Investor Report: Business Opportunity Summary Across 10 Digital Solutions for Assisted Living & Nursing Homes**

### *Prepared for prospective investors evaluating the senior-care technology market*

---

## **1. Care Task Automation & Family Transparency Portal**

**Category:** High-urgency operational efficiency tool
**Market Pain:** AL/SNF staff still use paper logs for ADLs, hydration, rounds, toileting. Families demand transparency.
**Core Opportunity:** Digitize the single most time-consuming daily workflow in senior care.
**Economic Value:**

* Facilities reduce labor waste and improve care compliance
* Eliminates documentation gaps that lead to survey citations
* Increases family satisfaction → boosts occupancy
  **Revenue Model:** $3–$5/resident/month or $299–$699 per building
  **Investor Take:** Strong wedge product; lowest development cost; fastest to adoption; ideal first module in a suite strategy.

---

## **2. Incident Reporting & Compliance Manager**

**Category:** High-risk regulatory workflow
**Market Pain:** Falls, injuries, and behavior events still documented manually or inconsistently. Poor reporting leads to citations, insurance risk, and lawsuits.
**Core Opportunity:** Create digital compliance that reduces liability.
**Economic Value:**

* Prevents costly fines and litigation through standardized investigations
* Reduces survey preparation time; ensures documentation completeness
* Provides corporate-level oversight across multiple facilities
  **Revenue Model:** $149–$499 per building/month
  **Investor Take:** Extremely sticky product with high ROI for operators; unlocks enterprise contracts; ideal second module.

---

## **3. Activity Management & Resident Engagement System**

**Category:** Quality-of-life and regulatory documentation
**Market Pain:** Activities teams lack tracking tools; participation documentation is often required by regulators.
**Core Opportunity:** Digitize activity calendars, attendance, and engagement analytics.
**Economic Value:**

* Improves care quality metrics
* Supports dementia care programming
* Enhances family satisfaction with engagement visibility
  **Revenue Model:** $1–$4/resident/month
  **Investor Take:** Low build cost, high cross-sell potential, strengthens suite stickiness.

---

## **4. Maintenance & Work Order Management**

**Category:** Operational efficiency & safety
**Market Pain:** Maintenance requests come via paper or text; no audit trail; safety issues cause survey deficiencies.
**Core Opportunity:** Bring facilities management software to a severely underserved sector.
**Economic Value:**

* Reduces downtime for rooms
* Ensures preventive maintenance compliance
* Lowers insurance and liability risks
  **Revenue Model:** $250–$2,000 per building/month
  **Investor Take:** Large market need + minimal competition in senior living; expands product footprint.

---

## **5. Staffing & Scheduling Optimization (Lite Version)**

**Category:** Labor management
**Market Pain:** Staffing is the #1 cost; current scheduling tools do not reflect acuity or care needs.
**Core Opportunity:** Predictive scheduling + simple shift management = major savings.
**Economic Value:**

* Reduces overtime and agency reliance
* Optimizes care hours per resident
* Boosts staff satisfaction and retention
  **Revenue Model:** $1,500–$8,000 per facility/month at scale
  **Investor Take:** Significant upside; requires more sophistication but high willingness to pay.

---

## **6. Resident Assessment & Care Plan Builder**

**Category:** Clinical decision support + compliance
**Market Pain:** Care plans are generic and outdated; assessments are time consuming and error-prone.
**Core Opportunity:** AI-assisted care plans tailored to resident needs.
**Economic Value:**

* Reduces nursing labor
* Improves survey readiness
* Standardizes documentation across locations
  **Revenue Model:** $5–$12/resident/month
  **Investor Take:** Higher complexity but extremely defensible IP; major differentiator long-term.

---

## **7. Infection Control & Outbreak Surveillance Dashboard**

**Category:** Post-COVID compliance + safety
**Market Pain:** Facilities must track symptoms, exposures, room movements, and infection trends, often manually.
**Core Opportunity:** Automated outbreak detection and centralized reporting.
**Economic Value:**

* Prevents costly outbreaks
* Simplifies compliance with CMS/state reporting
* Protects facility reputation
  **Revenue Model:** $1,000–$6,000 per enterprise/month
  **Investor Take:** Strategic module; powerful when combined with care tasks + incident data.

---

## **8. Predictive Staffing (AI/ML)**

**Category:** Predictive analytics
**Market Pain:** Staffing needs fluctuate based on acuity, incidents, and resident turnover; most scheduling is reactive.
**Core Opportunity:** Machine learning to forecast labor requirements.
**Economic Value:**

* Substantial reduction in overtime and agency staffing
* Aligns staffing with resident needs
* Massive cost savings for operators
  **Revenue Model:** $3,000–$15,000 per facility/month
  **Investor Take:** Premium, high-margin enterprise add-on; game-changer once dataset is established.

---

## **9. Medication Management / eMAR Enhancements**

**Category:** Clinical workflow
**Market Pain:** MAR errors cause regulatory penalties, hospitalizations, and lawsuits. Tools are outdated and cumbersome.
**Core Opportunity:** Build a modern MAR with real-time alerts and documentation support.
**Economic Value:**

* Prevents med errors and survey deficiencies
* Reduces staff training time
* Increases adherence and safety
  **Revenue Model:** $5–$10/resident/month
  **Investor Take:** Higher regulatory barrier; ideal only after gaining market traction with non-clinical modules.

---

## **10. Revenue Cycle / Billing & Eligibility Automation (RCM Lite)**

**Category:** Financial optimization
**Market Pain:** Private pay billing, Medicaid eligibility, co-pay tracking, and retroactive adjustments are extremely manual.
**Core Opportunity:** Automate the most complex and error-prone back-office process.
**Economic Value:**

* Reduces billing errors
* Improves cash flow
* Minimizes write-offs
* Supports Medicaid compliance
  **Revenue Model:** $500–$10,000 per facility/month depending on scale
  **Investor Take:** Very high revenue potential; requires integrations and deep domain modeling; better as a late-stage offering.

---

# **Portfolio-Level Investor Insight**

### **The strongest sequence for market capture and investor return is:**

1. **Care Task Automation (Module #1)** → fastest adoption, lowest risk
2. **Incident Reporting (Module #2)** → highly sticky, compliance-driven
3. **Activities or Maintenance Modules** → broadens operational footprint
4. **Staffing Lite** → begins labor optimization revenue
5. **Care Planning + Infection Control** → clinical-level moat
6. **Predictive Staffing + RCM Automation** → large enterprise expansion

### **Why this opportunity is compelling**

* The senior-care market is massive and technologically underserved
* Operational workflows (unlike EMR clinical systems) are wide open
* High switching costs = high retention
* Recurring revenue per building scales quickly
* Enterprise rollouts can multiply ARR dramatically
* Consolidation in senior living means strong acquisition paths
