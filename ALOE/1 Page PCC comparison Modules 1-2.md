# **Your Platform = OPERATIONS SYSTEM**

### *For Assisted Living & Memory Care day-to-day workflows*

Use **your platform** when the goal is:

* Making sure **care tasks get done**
* Tracking **ADLs, hydration, toileting, safety checks**
* Giving families **daily updates**
* Documenting **incidents** clearly and consistently
* Preparing **survey-ready compliance packets**
* Seeing **real-time status** of care delivery
* Reducing inbound family calls
* Training new caregivers quickly

**Best for:**
Frontline caregivers • Med techs • Shift leads • Care supervisors • Memory care teams • Executive Directors

**Why:**
It is simple, fast, mobile-friendly, and built for the **non-clinical operational side** of senior living.

---

# **PointClickCare = CLINICAL RECORD SYSTEM**

### *For Skilled Nursing and clinical documentation requirements*

Use **PointClickCare** when the goal is:

* Managing **EHR/clinical charting**
* Documenting **medications (eMAR)**
* Physician orders
* Diagnoses, assessments, and clinical histories
* Billing (SNF-focused)
* Medical records retention
* Regulatory SNF documentation

**Best for:**
Nurses • DONs • Clinical teams • MDS coordinators • Billing staff

**Why:**
PCC is built for the **clinical and regulatory** needs of skilled nursing — not for AL/MC operational workflows.

---

# **How They Work Together**

Most Assisted Living and Memory Care operators find the best setup is:

### **PointClickCare handles the clinical record**

(eMAR, assessments, notes, clinical documentation)

### **Your platform handles the daily care delivery**

(ADLs, rounds, hydration, family updates, incidents, compliance)

This creates a **complete picture** without overwhelming care staff with clinical systems.
