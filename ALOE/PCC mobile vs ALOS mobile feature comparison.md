# 📱 **Mobile / Point-of-Care Comparison**

### *PointClickCare (PCC) vs. AL Operating System (AL-OS)*

---

## **Summary**

PointClickCare *does* have mobile and point-of-care tools (Companion + POC), but they are built for **clinical/SNF workflows**, not **Assisted Living (AL) operational workflows**.
The AL-OS complements PCC by solving the **non-clinical, day-to-day operational needs** of AL/MC caregivers, families, maintenance, activity staff, and administrators.

PCC’s mobile tools support **clinical** workflows, not the everyday workflows AL caregivers actually perform.

### 2. **AL-OS is built for the 90% of AL work PCC doesn’t touch.**

Daily care tasks, family communication, incident workflows, engagement, maintenance, staffing, and non-clinical compliance.

### 3. **Both systems together create a complete picture.**

* **PCC = Clinical Record**
* **AL-OS = Operational Execution**

The two are not competitive; they are **complementary layers** of a full AL ecosystem.

### 4. **For a 300-building operation, AL-OS standardizes everything PCC does not.**

Tasks, incidents, activities, maintenance, staffing, billing assist, compliance, and family engagement become **uniform, measurable, and scalable**.

It means the organization is already comfortable with mobile charting, making AL-OS easier to adopt.
