## Step 1: Establish a realistic baseline usage

### Patients per OD

* 20 patients/day
* ~22 clinic days/month
  ➡️ **≈ 440 patients/month/OD**

### Eligibility calls per patient (average)

With your **staged eligibility strategy**:

* Primary eligibility only for most visits
* Secondary only sometimes

A **very reasonable planning average**:

* **1.2 Availity calls per patient**

(That assumes ~20% of patients trigger a second check.)

### Monthly calls per OD

```
440 patients × 1.2 calls ≈ 528 Availity calls / OD / month
```

### Your raw cost per OD

```
528 × $0.10 = $52.80 / OD / month
```

Round up for safety → **$60 / OD / month** cost exposure.

## Step 2: Decide how much you include in the base plan

You want the base plan to:

* cover **most “normal” usage**
* avoid nickel-and-diming
* still leave margin

A clean inclusion number:

* **500 Availity calls per OD per month included**

Cost to you:

```
500 × $0.10 = $50
```

## Step 3: Set a reasonable markup (this is key)

Healthcare SaaS norms for transactional infrastructure:

* **3×–6× markup** on underlying per-transaction costs is common
* Practices expect software value, not raw pass-through pricing

Let’s target:

* **~4× cost coverage** in the base subscription
