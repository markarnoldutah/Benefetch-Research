# 🌿 **ALOE**

### *Assisted Living Operating Environment*

**The calm, clear, modern way to run Assisted Living communities.**

---

## **What Is ALOE?**

ALOE is a purpose-built *operating system* for Assisted Living and Memory Care communities.
Where your clinical EMR (like PCC) handles documentation, **ALOE handles daily operations**—the tasks, workflows, and communication that keep your building running smoothly.

It is the layer that brings **clarity, consistency, and calm** to every shift.

---

## **What ALOE Solves**

### **1. Shift-to-Shift Chaos**

Care tasks, reminders, handoffs, and resident needs often live in paper notes, texts, or memory.
→ ALOE creates one unified place where every staff member sees exactly **what needs to be done, by whom, and when.**

### **2. Family Communication Overload**

Families expect updates, and staff often feel overwhelmed.
→ ALOE provides a structured, appropriate communication channel that keeps families informed *without disrupting care.*

### **3. Incident Management Gaps**

Incidents get missed, delayed, or tracked inconsistently.
→ ALOE ensures **real-time incident logging**, automatic follow-up, and leadership visibility.

### **4. Inconsistent Standards Across Locations**

Multi-site operators struggle to standardize workflows.
→ ALOE gives leadership a consistent, enterprise-ready operating environment for **every building**, without forcing culture change.

---

## **Why Operators Choose ALOE**

### ⭐ **It complements PCC, not replaces it**

ALOE handles everything PCC doesn’t—tasks, communication, shift rhythm, and building operations.

### ⭐ **Built for Assisted Living, not retrofitted from SNF**

ALOE fits the unique workflow of AL/MC teams: flexible, fast, and resident-experience driven.

### ⭐ **Easy to use for frontline staff**

Designed for caregivers with limited training time:

* Large buttons
* Simple workflows
* Clear priorities
* Mobile-friendly interface

### ⭐ **Enterprise visibility for leadership**

Operators get real-time insights into:

* Task completion
* Care quality patterns
* Incident trends
* Family communication
* Building-level compliance signals

### ⭐ **Consistency without micromanagement**

ALOE standardizes what should be consistent across buildings—while allowing each team to retain its feel.
