## **Overview**

This project launches a cloud-native, multi-tenant SaaS designed for Assisted Living (AL), Memory Care (MC), and Skilled Nursing (SNF) operators. The platform addresses two of the industry's highest-pain operational challenges:

### **Module #1 — Care Task Automation + Family Transparency**

A mobile-first solution that replaces paper ADL logs, hydration checks, rounds, toileting schedules, and shift reports. It offers real-time updates to administrators and families, reducing staffing burden and increasing satisfaction.

### **Module #2 — Incident Reporting + Compliance Manager**

A regulatory-focused workflow engine for documenting falls, skin tears, behaviors, MAR errors, and corrective actions. State-ready export packets reduce survey citations and legal exposure.

Both modules share a common foundation:

* .NET + Azure App Service
* Azure SQL (multi-tenant)
* Blazor Web/MAUI mobile
* Azure Blob storage for photo documentation
* Optional Azure AD B2C identity

Together, they form the core of a broader “Facility Operations Suite” that can expand into staffing, activities, maintenance, billing, and assessments.
