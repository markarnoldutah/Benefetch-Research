# **FEATURES BY USER PERSONA — Incident Reporting + Compliance Manager**

---

# **1. Caregivers (Frontline Staff)**

**Primary Goal:** Capture incident information quickly, accurately, and with minimal complexity.

### **Key Features for Caregivers**

#### **✔ 1. Rapid Incident Creation**

* One-tap “Create Incident” on mobile
* Auto-stamps time, resident, staff, location

#### **✔ 2. Guided Incident Forms**

* Simple, step-by-step questions
* Dropdowns for fall/skin tear/behavior/etc.
* Required fields highlighted so nothing is missed

#### **✔ 3. Photo Capture & Upload**

* Take photos directly in the app
* Automatically attaches to the report
* Supports multiple photos (scene, injury, environment)

#### **✔ 4. Injury Body Map**

* Tap-to-mark injury location
* Reduces charting errors
* Helps nurses assess severity quickly

#### **✔ 5. Add Notes or Voice-to-Text**

* Quick casual language allowed
* Converts to structured notes for the incident timeline

#### **✔ 6. “My Incidents” Dashboard**

* See incidents they’ve documented
* Track which ones require follow-up or additional details

#### **Value for Caregivers**

* Less paperwork
* Faster documentation
* Reduced fear of making mistakes
* Clear instructions on what they must complete

---

# **2. Nurses / Med Techs (Clinical Role)**

**Primary Goal:** Review incidents, complete investigations, make medical judgments, and ensure accuracy.

### **Features for Nurses**

#### **✔ 1. Incident Review Queue**

* All new incidents flow into their dashboard
* Prioritized by severity and type

#### **✔ 2. Clinical Investigation Workflow**

* Root cause analysis templates
* Vitals, pain level, neurological checks
* Interventions & monitoring notes

#### **✔ 3. Witness Interviews & Documentation**

* Add statements
* Sign-off capture with timestamp

#### **✔ 4. Medical Follow-Up Tracking**

* Track neuro checks, wound care, behavior follow-up
* Automated reminders for repeated checks

#### **✔ 5. Medication-Related Errors Log**

* Non-MAR based documentation (no EMR integration required)
* Track: late, missed, wrong dose, refusal

#### **✔ 6. Corrective Action Assignment**

* Assign tasks to caregivers or maintenance
* Due dates + alerts

#### **✔ 7. Nurse-Specific Reports**

* Fall trends
* Repeat resident incidents
* Behavior escalation patterns

#### **Value for Nurses**

* Accurate, complete records
* Reduced survey risk
* Easier oversight of patterns and follow-ups

---

# **3. Administrators / Executive Directors**

**Primary Goal:** Ensure the facility meets regulatory requirements, stays survey-ready, and reduces risk.

### **Features for Administrators**

#### **✔ 1. Facility Incident Dashboard**

* Fall rates, behavior trends, med errors
* Filters by resident, shift, staff member
* Heatmap by time of day or location

#### **✔ 2. State-Ready PDF Export Packets**

* Automatically generates survey-ready documents
* Includes: notes, photos, body maps, follow-up, corrective actions

#### **✔ 3. Compliance Workflow Oversight**

* See which incidents are incomplete
* Track overdue investigations
* Monitor staff performance

#### **✔ 4. Trend Analysis Reports**

* Month-to-month comparison
* Incident per occupied bed (IOB) ratio
* Reports for board, owners, or regional leadership

#### **✔ 5. Automatic Alerts**

* For severe events
* For repeated falls
* For missing follow-ups
* For potential abuse/neglect patterns

#### **✔ 6. Environmental Hazard Tracking**

* Connects maintenance issues with incidents
* Shows correlation between hazards & resident risk

#### **Value for Administrators**

* Reduced regulatory citations
* Quick survey responses
* Improved communication with corporate
* Better understanding of staffing issues

---

# **4. Regional / Corporate Compliance Staff**

**Primary Goal:** Oversee multiple buildings, ensure consistent documentation, and reduce liability.

### **Features for Corporate Compliance**

#### **✔ 1. Multi-Facility Dashboard**

* Compare all buildings in one view
* Identify trouble facilities
* Drill into specific incidents

#### **✔ 2. Benchmarking & Comparison**

* Per-facility fall rate
* Incident types by region
* Staff performance trends
* Time-to-close investigations

#### **✔ 3. Corporate Review Queue**

* Escalated incidents
* Serious injuries
* High-risk residents
* Behavior events requiring external reporting

#### **✔ 4. Standardized Templates & Forms**

* Corporate can push updated workflows
* Ensures all facilities meet standards

#### **✔ 5. Corporate-Level Export Packets**

* For audits
* Legal reviews
* Insurance claims
* Root cause assessments

#### **Value for Corporate Teams**

* Dramatically lower risk exposure
* Standardization across locations
* Corporate oversight without micromanagement

---

# **5. Family Members (Optional & Limited)**

**Note:** Families do *not* see full incident reports due to privacy & regulatory constraints.

### **Family Features**

#### **✔ “Event Occurred” Notification** (non-clinical)

* “Your loved one experienced an incident today. The nurse will update you shortly.”

#### **✔ High-Level Summary** (MVP+1 feature)

* Date, time, and reassurance
* “Resident safe and being monitored.”

#### **✔ Staff Messaging**

* Ask questions directly through secure messaging

#### **Value for Families**

* Reduced anxiety
* Improved transparency (without violating clinical boundaries)

---

# **6. Owners / Operators / Investors**

**Primary Goal:** Improve oversight, decrease risk, and increase occupancy through compliance excellence.

### **Features for Owners**

#### **✔ 1. Risk Dashboard (Portfolio-Level)**

* Facility risk score
* Incident hotspots
* Outliers (best and worst performers)

#### **✔ 2. Quarterly Reports**

* Trend summaries
* Compliance KPIs
* Time-to-close investigations
* Survey-preparation indicators

#### **✔ 3. Liability Reduction Insights**

* Identify patterns before litigation emerges
* Correlate staffing → incidents
* Correlate environment → safety

#### **✔ 4. Facility Health Score**

* Composite score combining:

  * incidents
  * follow-up timeliness
  * resident risk profile
  * staff compliance

#### **Value for Owners**

* Better compliance = higher valuation
* Lower insurance premiums
* Stronger occupancy & family satisfaction
* Better market reputation
