## 1. How optometry practices mentally bucket software costs

Front-desk and revenue-cycle tools tend to fall into **three psychological price bands** for ODs:

### 🟢 “No-brainer add-on”

* **$49–$149 / OD / month**
* Examples: small integrations, reminders, analytics widgets
* Expectation: limited scope, minimal support

### 🟡 “Operational core tool”

* **$150–$300 / OD / month**
* Examples: eligibility, claims helpers, scheduling optimizers
* Expectation:

  * saves staff time
  * reduces mistakes
  * touches revenue
  * *worth it if it works*

### 🔴 “Enterprise / PM replacement territory”

* **$300–$600+ / OD / month**
* Examples: full PM/EHR modules, revenue cycle outsourcing
* Expectation:

  * deep integration
  * contracts
  * heavy onboarding

👉 **$199 sits squarely in the “operational core tool” band**, which is exactly where eligibility belongs.

## 2. How competitors *actually* price (even if they don’t say it clearly)

Most competitors obscure true pricing, but functionally they do one of the following:

### Pattern A — Bundle eligibility into a bigger PM fee

* Eligibility is “included”
* But PM fees are already **$400–$700 / OD / month**
* Customers don’t see eligibility as “free”; it’s just hidden

### Pattern B — Per-transaction / per-claim fees

* $0.10–$0.35 per eligibility or claim
* Often **unpredictable**
* Practices hate surprise bills

### Pattern C — Monthly + bucket + overage

* Common in clearinghouse-adjacent tools
* Usually **$150–$300/month base**
* Overages quietly charged

👉 Your model is **already familiar**, even if competitors don’t explain it cleanly.

## 4. Pass-through overage is actually a plus (if framed correctly)

### What practices fear

* surprise bills
* “gotcha” pricing
* unclear thresholds

### What your model communicates

* “Most practices never pay overage”
* “If you do, it’s because you used more”
* “We’re not marking it up secretly”

This is *especially acceptable* when:

* base plan includes **most normal usage**
* overage is **clearly disclosed**
* you show usage transparently in-app

Psychologically, this is **less threatening** than a higher flat fee.
